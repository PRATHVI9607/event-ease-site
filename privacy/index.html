Event Ease Privacy Policy

Effective date: 22 October 2026

Event Ease is an app for booking community and event halls. It is run by Prasad, Nihal, Sagar and Preetham, Acharigundi Thota, Bankerkatta, Ambalpady, Udupi, Karnataka 576101 ("we", "us").

This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, who sees it, and how you control it. Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act), we are the "Data Fiduciary" and you are the "Data Principal".

1. The short version

  • We collect only what we need to run bookings, payment proofs, refunds and hall listings.
  • Event Ease never receives, holds or moves your money. You pay the booking advance directly to the hall manager's UPI ID, using your own UPI app.
  • We never see or store your UPI PIN, card number, CVV, bank password or bank login.
  • We do not sell your data. We show no ads. We do no ad tracking.
  • Your location stays on your phone.
  • Ratings of 1 to 3 stars are private to the hall's management. Ratings of 4 or 5 stars may be public.
  • You can see, fix or delete your data. You can withdraw consent at any time.

2. Who can use Event Ease

Event Ease is only for people aged 18 or over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If we learn that a child has made an account, we will delete it.

3. What we collect and why

If you use Event Ease (everyone)

  • Name, email and phone number. To create your account, sign you in, send booking updates and let the hall contact you about your booking.
  • Password. Handled by our sign-in provider (Supabase Auth). It is stored only in a scrambled (hashed) form. We never see it.
  • Google sign-in details (name, email, Google account ID), if you choose "Sign in with Google". To create and sign in to your account.
  • Two-step verification (2FA) secret, if you turn on an authenticator app. To protect your account. 2FA is required for admins.
  • Profile photo (optional). To show on your profile.
  • Booking details: hall, dates and time slot, event type, guest count, contact name and phone, and any notes. To make, confirm, change and cancel your booking, and to give the hall what it needs to host your event.
  • Reviews and ratings you write, including earlier versions if you edit one. To show feedback to the hall and, for 4 or 5 stars, to other users (see section 5).
  • Reports you make about a review, and the reason. To moderate reviews.
  • Notifications and push token. To send booking updates, reminders and alerts to your phone.
  • App preferences, such as which notifications you want and light or dark mode. To respect your choices.
  • Change history (audit log). A record of important changes, such as prices, booking status, payment confirmations, refunds and role changes, and who made them. To prevent fraud, settle disputes and keep the service safe.
  • Technical and security logs, such as request times and IP address, kept by our hosting provider. To keep the service secure, detect misuse and fix problems.
  • Crash reports (only if crash reporting is turned on): device model, Android version, app version and what the app was doing when it crashed. To find and fix bugs. We set up crash reporting so that it does not include your name, email, phone, booking details, UPI IDs or payment proofs.

Payments and refunds (customers)

Event Ease does not process payments. You pay the advance from your own UPI app to the hall manager's UPI ID. You pay the balance at the hall. To make this work, we collect:

  • UTR (Unique Transaction Reference). The 12-digit number your UPI app shows after you pay. To let the manager match your payment in their bank app.
  • Payment screenshot. A picture of the payment success screen from your UPI app. To prove payment and to settle disputes. Before you upload it, please hide your bank balance and full account number. We only need the amount, date and time, payee, and UTR.
  • Payment status and history: amount due, when you submitted proof, and whether the manager confirmed or rejected it, with any reason. To track your booking and settle disputes.
  • Your UPI ID for refunds, when a refund is due. So the manager can send your refund.
  • Refund records: refund amount (worked out by the app from the hall's cancellation policy), the refund UTR the manager enters, dates, and any "refund not received" report you make, with messages between you, the manager and our admins. To track refunds and help settle disputes.

If you list a hall (managers)

  • Hall details: name, type, description, contact phone, address, map location, photos, prices, time slots, blocked dates, facilities, rules and cancellation policy. To show your hall to customers and take bookings.
  • UPI ID, payee name and UPI QR code. So customers can pay the booking advance directly to you. A UPI ID can contain your phone number or name, so choose one you are comfortable sharing with paying customers.
  • Your payment and refund actions: which payments you confirmed or rejected, refunds you marked as sent and their refund UTRs. To keep a clear record for customers and disputes.

We do not collect your bank account number, IFSC, PAN or other bank or KYC details. If the law later requires us to collect tax details, we will update this policy first.

Location

  • We use your phone's location only when you tap "Nearest" to sort halls, or "Use current location" when adding a hall's address.
  • For sorting, your location is used for that one search. We do not store it on our servers.
  • You can deny or turn off location permission at any time in your phone settings. The rest of the app still works.

Legal basis

We process most data with your consent, which you give when you sign up. We keep booking, payment-proof and refund records, and processing logs, where the law requires it or allows it for legitimate uses, such as preventing fraud, handling disputes and responding to lawful requests.

4. Information stored on your phone

To work faster and offline, the app keeps a copy of recent data on your phone. This includes halls you viewed, your bookings, receipts and notifications, for up to 7 days. It is stored encrypted. Signing out clears it.

5. Who can see your data

  • The public can see verified hall pages. This includes the hall's name, address, map location, photos, prices and other details the manager adds. Our admins check halls before they become public.
  • Public reviews. If you give 4 or 5 stars, your rating and comment may be shown publicly after a basic check. It shows only your first name, last initial (for example "Priya S.") and the month and year. Ratings of 1 to 3 stars, and their comments, are never public. They are shared only with that hall's management and our admins.
  • Hall managers see your name and booking details for bookings at their hall. They see your phone number only once the booking is confirmed or completed. They see the UTR and payment screenshot you submit for their hall, and your UPI ID when they owe you a refund.
  • Customers paying a hall see that hall's UPI ID, payee name and QR code. These are shown only to customers whose booking at that hall is waiting for payment, not to the public.
  • Our admins see what they need to verify halls, moderate reviews and help with payment or refund problems. This includes UTRs, payment screenshots and refund records. Admins must use 2FA.
  • Payment screenshots are stored privately. Only you, the manager of the hall you booked, and our admins can open them.
  • Nobody, including us, can see your UPI PIN, card number, CVV or bank password. We never receive them.

When you tap "Pay", Event Ease opens your chosen UPI app with the hall's UPI ID and the amount filled in. The payment happens inside that UPI app, under its own terms and privacy policy. Event Ease does not receive your payment data from it.

6. Service providers we use

We share data only with these providers, and only so they can do their job for us:

  • Supabase: database, sign-in, file storage (including payment screenshots) and server functions.
  • Google: "Sign in with Google", if you use it.
  • Expo and Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google): delivering push notifications and app updates.
  • Sentry: crash reports, only if crash reporting is turned on. No personal data is included (see section 3).
  • GitHub: app downloads and updates, and encrypted database backups.

Your data is stored with Supabase in Mumbai, India. Expo, Google, GitHub and Sentry may process some data outside India, for example push tokens, sign-in details, encrypted backups and technical crash data. We transfer data outside India only to countries the Government of India has not restricted under the DPDP Act, and only with providers bound to protect it.

We may also share data if the law requires it, for example with tax authorities, courts or the police under a valid order.

7. How long we keep it

  • Your account: while your account is active.
  • Booking, payment-proof and refund records (including UTRs, payment screenshots, your refund UPI ID and refund UTRs): to settle disputes, prevent fraud and meet legal duties, generally up to 8 years after the booking ends, or longer if a dispute or legal case is still open. This applies even after you delete your account. After that, we delete them.
  • Processing and security logs: at least 1 year where the law requires it, including the DPDP Rules, and then deleted unless needed for an open investigation or dispute.
  • Old notifications: about 180 days.
  • Backups: 14 days.
  • Copies on your phone: up to 7 days, or until you sign out.

8. Deleting your account

Go to Profile → Delete account.

  • You must have signed in recently.
  • You cannot delete your account while you have a live booking, payment or refund. Cancel it or wait until it is over.
  • When you delete, we remove your login and anonymise your profile, so your name, email, phone and photo no longer link to you.
  • Your reviews, their earlier versions and your entries in the change history (audit log) are anonymised, so they no longer identify you. Ratings may still count towards a hall's score.
  • If you are a manager, your halls are taken off the app and your UPI ID and QR code are removed.
  • We keep booking, payment-proof and refund records, and logs, for the periods in section 7. After that, we delete them.

9. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you can:

  • Access: get a summary of your personal data, what we do with it, and who we have shared it with.
  • Correct, complete or update data that is wrong or missing. You can edit your name, phone, photo and (for managers) UPI details in the app.
  • Erase your data. Use "Delete account", or write to us. We may keep data the law requires us to keep.
  • Withdraw consent at any time (see section 10).
  • Nominate someone to use these rights for you if you die or cannot act yourself.
  • Raise a grievance with us (see section 12).

To use any right, email [GRIEVANCE EMAIL] from your account email, or use Profile → Help. We may ask you to confirm who you are. We will reply within 30 days.

10. Withdrawing consent

Withdrawing consent is as easy as giving it:

  • Push notifications: turn them off in the app's notification settings or your phone settings.
  • Location: turn off location permission in your phone settings.
  • Everything else: delete your account (section 8), or email [GRIEVANCE EMAIL].

After you withdraw, we stop using your data for that purpose. Anything done before stays lawful. If you withdraw consent for data we need to run bookings, you will not be able to keep using the app.

11. How we protect your data

  • All connections between the app and our servers are encrypted.
  • Your sign-in session and offline data are encrypted on your phone.
  • Strict access rules inside our database decide who can see each record.
  • Payment screenshots are kept in private storage. Each link to open one works only for a short time and only for the people listed in section 5.
  • Admins must use two-step verification.
  • Backups are encrypted.

We follow reasonable security practices under the Information Technology Act, 2000 and its rules. No system is perfectly secure. If a data breach affects you, we will tell you and the Data Protection Board of India, as the law requires.
