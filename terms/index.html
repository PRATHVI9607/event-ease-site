5. How booking works

  1. You choose a hall, a date and a time slot. The app shows the price, the advance, the balance and the hall's cancellation policy before you book.
  2. Some halls confirm instantly. Others need the manager to approve your request first.
  3. Once your booking is ready for payment, the slot is held for you for a limited time shown in the app. If you do not pay and submit proof in that time, the hold ends and the slot is released.
  4. You pay the advance by UPI and submit proof (section 6).
  5. The manager checks the payment and confirms or rejects it. Your booking is confirmed only when the manager confirms your payment.
  6. You pay the balance at the hall, as agreed with the manager.

Prices are in Indian rupees. The price shown when you book is the price that applies to that booking.

6. Advance payment by UPI

  • The app shows the manager's UPI ID, payee name and QR code only while your booking is waiting for payment. You can tap to open your UPI app, or scan the QR code.
  • Before you pay, check that the payee name in your UPI app matches the name shown in Event Ease. If it does not match, do not pay. Contact us.
  • Pay the exact advance amount shown.
  • After paying, submit the 12-digit UTR (Unique Transaction Reference) and a screenshot of the payment success screen. Hide your bank balance and full account number in the screenshot.
  • The manager checks their own bank or UPI app and then confirms or rejects. Event Ease does not check payments with any bank.
  • If the manager rejects your proof but you have paid, raise a dispute through Profile → Help. A manager must return any money they received for a booking that is not confirmed.
  • Event Ease is not responsible for failed, delayed or wrong UPI payments, or for payments sent to the wrong UPI ID.

Fake proof is not allowed. Submitting a fake, edited or someone else's UTR or screenshot, or falsely rejecting a real payment, will lead to suspension or closure of your account. We may also report it to the police and take legal action.
